# Flagstaff — Project Overview

_Captured 2026-10-09 from Ghanii's initial brief. Product answers added 2026-10-12._

## What it is
A social media management platform with an AI agent at its core, built for the music industry.

## Primary users (for now)
Fan accounts: people who run social accounts dedicated to their favorite music artists and support those fandoms.
- **Multi-account:** users can manage several accounts.

## Core purpose
Help fan accounts grow engagement on their social accounts.

## Platforms
- **Launch:** X (Twitter) only.
- **Later:** other social platforms will be connected in the future.

## The agent: how much it does
An intelligent assistant that acts proactively. It drafts posts, schedules them, publishes them, and suggests what to post and what not to post. It is the main part of the product, not a side feature.

## Key capabilities
- **Content suggestions**: what to post, when to post, how to post, and what to avoid
- **Drafting, scheduling and publishing**: done by the agent itself
- **Trend alerts**: notify the user when a relevant topic is trending so they can post about it
- **Campaigns**: a set of posts scheduled around an upcoming event or toward a goal, for example a release or birthday. That is one example; a campaign can be anything else as well.
- **Analytics**: show how posts are performing, plus recommendations to improve results

## Ghanii's role
Designer on the project, and also responsible for how the agent performs and behaves.

## Brand
- **Logo:** four purple circles in a 2×2 grid
- **Palette (still being explored):** a primary indigo (about #5A5DD9) and a light grey/lavender neutral scale. Ramps were explored for Grey, Purple, Matte Purple, Pastel Purple and Loom Purple. The hex values here are estimates read from screenshots; replace them with the real tokens from Figma.
- **Rule:** mascot fills stay within the palette and read as indigo, not blue.

## Agent mascot — DECIDED: Direction 1 (logo blob), 2026-10-12
- **Final mascot:** a soft four-lobed indigo blob (top, left, right and bottom lobes), with two white vertical pill eyes and an indigo pill line below it.
- **Concept:** the mascot comes from the logo. The four logo circles rotate 45° so they sit at top, left, right and bottom, then expand and connect into the body. The eyes blink open and the line appears.
- **Full details:** the formation animation and all mood states are in `flagstaff-mascot-moods.md`. The files are `flagstaff-mascot-motion-v1.html` and `flagstaff-mascot-moods-v1.html`.

### Not chosen — Direction 2 (visor helmet), kept for reference
- Explored in v1–v4 (`flagstaff-mascot-visor-v1…v4.html`): a smooth round helmet with a visor opening showing two eyes, tried in matte and glossy finishes, several colourways and glasses styles. Direction 3 is also documented in `flagstaff-mascot-direction3.md`.
